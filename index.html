YOU JUST SCANNED A QR CODE

Would you have checked where it was taking you?

QR codes are convenient, but they hide the destination link until you scan. That makes them useful for QR phishing, or “quishing.”

See what the research found

THE NUMBERS MATTER

Even tech-savvy users can miss the warning signs.

85%

of users in a computing building scanned without checking first.

92%

continued when the phishing URL was shortened in the preview.

56%

continued when the URL contained an obvious word such as “phish.”

19%

said it was difficult to see the entire URL on a mobile browser.

!

The poster around a QR code does not prove the code is safe.

A fundraiser, raffle, free gift, or workplace notice can create a believable reason to scan. Pause and inspect the destination before opening it.
